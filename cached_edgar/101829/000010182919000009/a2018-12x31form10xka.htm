EXPLANATORY NOTE
This Amendment No. 1 on Form 10-K/A amends United Technologies Corporation’s Annual Report on Form 10-K for the year ended December 31, 2018, which the Company previously filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on February 7, 2019. The Company is filing this amendment for the sole purpose of including the information required by Item 201(d) of Regulation S-K in Part III, Item 12, which information had been inadvertently omitted from the Form 10-K.
